The figure guard, canaries and the model port arrive in the core: `stripUnauthorizedNumbers`, `StreamingNumberGuard`, `findLeaks` and `hasLeak` read figures through a `FigureClass` (`moneyFigures` is the default and behaves exactly as before), and take the replacement text from the pack (`DEFAULT_REPLACEMENTS` for English and French). `createCanaries` plants and finds reference codes. `LlmPort`, `streamTurn`, `LlmProviderError` and `llmPortContractCases`, the framework-free contract any adapter runs. `/adapters/gemini` ships `GeminiLlmAdapter`; `/adapters/dummy` ships a scripted `DummyLlmAdapter` a pack drives with its own script.
